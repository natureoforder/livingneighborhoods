The original site offered a printed workbook and workpads for taking the steps onto the land, and an online drawing pad for articulating each unfolding. The drawing pad was a Java applet, which modern browsers no longer run.

## The workbook

- [The workbook](workbook.md): this workbook can serve to help you think about and implement the Unfoldings described on this website in a step by step manner for your project, and to organize the information you generate with others. It covers what goes in the workbook, instructions, downloads, assembly and printing.
- [Workbook section 1: Startup and vision](workbook-section-1.md): the contents of the first section of the workbook, with its five unfolding pages and complementary worksheets.
- [Workpad for startup and vision](workpad-startup-and-vision.md): the first workpad, beginning with the basic worksheet of facts about your project.

## The drawing pad

- [The drawing pad](drawing-pad.md): this drawing pad can be used to articulate each unfolding.
