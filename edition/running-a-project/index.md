The people, money and contracts side of building a living neighborhood: the main steps of work, creating community, working with developers, the construction contracts, and how construction is organised. The steps of the generative code itself are in [Generative code](../generative-code/index.md).

## The overall process

- [Main steps of work](main-steps-of-work.md): the main phases of a project, each a distinct constellation of issues which come into play in approximate sequence.
- [Construction organisation](construction-organisation.md): setting up a design/management/construction organization for a large project, with a worked example from *The Nature of Order*.

## People and community

- [Creating community](creating-community.md): if one thing, more than any other, distinguishes a real neighborhood from the corporate machine-architecture of the 20th-century developer, it is the fact that a real neighborhood is a community.
- [For developers](for-developers.md): most developers are idealistic people who wish to make a difference, who are proud to be spending their lives building communities for others.
- [A developers' charter](developers-charter.md): a first draft of a charter for professional developers.

## Contracts

- [The main construction contract](main-contract.md): an agreement between the Employer and the Center for Environmental Structure, acting as Management Contractor and General Contractor of record.
- [The craftsperson's subcontract](craftsperson-subcontract.md): an agreement that seeks to obtain the best possible work for the owner by obtaining design and construction work from the craftsman.
