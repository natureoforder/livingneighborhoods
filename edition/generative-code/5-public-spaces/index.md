**Identify and locate major and minor centers which will work well as public places.** This phase includes steps to shape, in outline form, the whole global configuration of public spaces in the neighborhood.

## The steps

1. **[Pedestrians always first](pedestrians-first.md).** As a general matter, public spaces for pedestrians must be defined *before*, not after, defining the spaces for cars, roads and parking.
2. **[Main center](main-center.md).** Then the first concrete thing to do is to find the most natural place for the neighborhood's main center.
3. **Embellish.** Embellish the main center with places, and music, and dance around it. *(Never written up on the original site.)*
4. **Enlarge.** If a public place becomes successful, enlarge it. *(Never written up.)*
5. **Paths.** Locate the paths along the most beautiful spots and lines. *(Never written up; see [magic configurations](../2-diagnosis/magic-configurations.md) in the diagnosis.)*
6. **Minor centers.** Locate minor public centers as far as makes sense at this early stage. *(Never written up.)*
7. **[Positive space](positive-space.md).** Go back over all the public spaces located so far, and give each one a size and shape appropriate to its position. Step by step, you are beginning to shape the shared space of the community.
8. **[Recursive positive space](recursive-positive-space.md).** Make sophisticated public spaces by applying positive space again and again.
