<td style="text-align: left;" width="40%" data-valign="top"><span class="h404class"> PROJECT HISTORY</span> <span class="h4class">Seventy families: we taught them to build a neighborhood for themselves, in the mountains of central Colombia. Originally it started as a project of Construyamos, one of the largest self help housing cooperatives in Latin America, with an excellent track record of building mass-produced, low-cost housing. Their usual plan was to have eighteen families at a time, forming a group. An architect would draw up a standard house, repeated eighteen times, identically repeated. </span> <span class="CAPTION">Hillside, showing Santa Rose families working to lay out their streets and houses.</span>
