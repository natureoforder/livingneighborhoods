<td style="text-align: left;" data-valign="top" width="20%"><span class="h404class">IS LAND CHOSEN OR NOT YET CHOSEN?</span></td>
<td style="text-align: left;" width="80%"><span class="blackChange"><span class="h4class">Two different scenarios:</span></span>
<ol>
<li>Site is identified. People have a piece of land and have an idea of what they want to do there. This is the typical case.</li>
<li>Site is not yet identified. People have an idea of what they want to do but haven't yet found a piece of land where they feel it's appropriate to do what they have in mind. This is a less common case that ought to be encouraged. We can give help to people who are simply trying to articulate what it is they hope to accomplish, without knowing the site they will do it on, and then help advise on purchase of land which fits the picture which has begun to emerge.</li>
