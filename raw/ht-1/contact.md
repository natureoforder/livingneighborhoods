<span class="h32class"><span style="FONT-SIZE:80%"><span id="#ANCHOR"></span>WE HOPE THE MATERIALS ON THIS WEBSITE SUPPORT THE EFFORTS OF YOUR COMMUNITY TO BUILD, OR REBUILD, YOUR NEIGHBORHOOD. <span class="h404class">WRITE TO US</span>

We are very interested in learning about the projects you are working on, especially if you believe you might need some advice. Completing the [worksheet](worksheet1.htm) on the Work Plan page will provide us with the information we need to be of assistance to you.

<span class="h404class">STAFF</span>

<span class="h4class">Phone numbers and email addresses of our associates.</span>

<span class="h404class">HELP AND SERVICES</span>

<span class="h4class">An overview of the [types of help](services.htm) we offer, appears on the Services page. Possibilities range from low cost assistance, to building design, to full professional services for building large-scale or small neighborhoods.</span>

<span class="h404class">TIME SCALE</span>

<span class="h4class">A rough outline of the [time scales](timescale.htm) that are needed to accomplish projects of different orders of magnitued with us.</span>

<span class="h404class">REGISTER AS A MEMBER</span>

<span class="h4class">We have a list of people who are currently receiving help from us, and who are trying, in their own way, to build innovative forms of neighborhood. We value all these people, and would be very glad [if you would like to add your name](member.htm), or your firm's name, to the list. If you provide your email address, we will notify you whenever new materials are added to this website</span>.
