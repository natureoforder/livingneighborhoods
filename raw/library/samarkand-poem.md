<p><strong><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Palatino Linotype&#39;; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">1. It is a sequence of public squares, gardens, and buildings, which will form the new center of the city of Samarkand, uniting historic and traditional buildings and quarters.</span></strong><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt"></span></p>
<p><strong><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Palatino Linotype&#39;; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">2. </span><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Book Antiqua&#39;; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">There is a new dimension here, a center of spiri­tual life. It is not a commercial center, not a cultural center, not a religious center in the old idea. It is not a convention center. Somehow, this new center of the city of Samarkand, unites old and new, weaves to­gether the thread of the silk road, the tomb of Timur the Great, with the modern world, and a vision of the world in which comfortable human concern, and a spiritual awareness of the importance of life, is vis­ible, felt, and active.<span style="LETTER-SPACING: 1pt"></span></span></strong></p>
<p><strong><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Palatino Linotype&#39;; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">3.</span><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Book Antiqua&#39;; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt"> It is an inspiring place to go. A place of pilgrimage, which will receive visitors from the five continents, in increasing thousands.</span><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Palatino Linotype&#39;; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt"></span></strong></p>
<p><strong><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Palatino Linotype&#39;; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">4. </span><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Book Antiqua&#39;; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">A network of beautiful paths, formed by columns, colonnades, brick walls, buildings, gardens. This network of paths, which passes across the whole area, is formed by the building masses which arise out of it, and by formal gardens.</span></strong><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt"></span></p>
<p><strong><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Palatino Linotype&#39;; LETTER-SPACING: 1pt; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">5. </span><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Book Antiqua&#39;; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">Do the paths open into courtyards, ponds, gardens, hidden places? Are they formed only by mysterious buildings, rising in color, tile, and marble? Are there figures, statues, animals, Gods, people, statues stand­ing at the places where the paths cross?<span style="LETTER-SPACING: 1pt"></span></span></strong></p>
<p><strong><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Palatino Linotype&#39;; LETTER-SPACING: 1pt; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">6. </span><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Book Antiqua&#39;; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">Are the animals themselves covered with mysteri­ous animals?</span><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Palatino Linotype&#39;; LETTER-SPACING: 1pt; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt"></span></strong></p>
<p><strong><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Palatino Linotype&#39;; LETTER-SPACING: 1pt; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">7. </span><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Book Antiqua&#39;; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">Is there any reference to voyages?</span><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Palatino Linotype&#39;; LETTER-SPACING: 1pt; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt"></span></strong></p>
<p><strong><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Palatino Linotype&#39;; LETTER-SPACING: 1pt; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">8. </span><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Book Antiqua&#39;; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">The main thing one is aware of is a network of green and beautiful jewel-like streets. Each has lush trees, seats, platforms, streams.</span><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Palatino Linotype&#39;; LETTER-SPACING: 1pt; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt"></span></strong></p>
<p><strong><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Palatino Linotype&#39;; LETTER-SPACING: 1pt; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">9. </span><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Book Antiqua&#39;; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">These green streets, made by their trees, benches, sitting platforms, and edges, form a lacework of places to walk. They are like parks, long and nar­row, you can explore for many hours, walking around these streets.</span><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Palatino Linotype&#39;; LETTER-SPACING: 1pt; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt"></span></strong></p>
<p><strong><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Palatino Linotype&#39;; LETTER-SPACING: 1pt; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">10. </span><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Book Antiqua&#39;; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">Each one of the streets arrives on some new trea­sure. Each building is like a treasure, arrived at by the green streets.</span><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Palatino Linotype&#39;; LETTER-SPACING: 1pt; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt"></span></strong></p>
<p><strong><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Palatino Linotype&#39;; LETTER-SPACING: 1pt; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">11.</span><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Book Antiqua&#39;; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt"> Samarkand, historically, and in the time of Ulugh Beg, was a crossroads of the world. In the Tang dynasty period, every conceivable exotic sub­stance, or idea, or artifact, or art on earth, came through Samarkand. No matter where it went, or where it came from, it went through Samarkand.</span><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Palatino Linotype&#39;; LETTER-SPACING: 1pt; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt"></span></strong></p>
<p><strong><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Palatino Linotype&#39;; LETTER-SPACING: 1pt; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">12. </span><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Book Antiqua&#39;; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">Somehow, then, one may imagine these green heavenly paths, as a network�almost a mythical bazaar in which reference to these many exotic sub­stances exists.</span></strong></p>
<p><strong><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Palatino Linotype&#39;; LETTER-SPACING: 1pt; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">13. </span><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Book Antiqua&#39;; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">The blue tile work of the Timurids, the hand-painted blue tiles, with small black, yellow, and white detail, on mud brick�these tiles, and the yel­low bricks are in evidence on walls, domes, court­yards throughout the center. It is a thread which connects.</span><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Palatino Linotype&#39;; LETTER-SPACING: 1pt; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt"></span></strong></p>
<p><strong><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Palatino Linotype&#39;; LETTER-SPACING: 1pt; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">14. </span><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Book Antiqua&#39;; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">The whole network of paths is almost like a forbidden city. A place which is walled, punctured at very occasional places which allow one to enter, a special area that contains its own magic.</span><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Palatino Linotype&#39;; LETTER-SPACING: 1pt; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt"></span></strong></p>
