<span class="h4class" style="color:black">**WHAT DOES WRITING POETRY GET YOU?** Some people are reluctant to try writing poetry. If you feel that way, please be reassured that there is a good reason for such an attempt. We are looking for word "pictures" that have feelings in them - that describe reality in ways beyond analytical and technical ways of thinking. Writing poetry gives you permission to talk about things freely, in this feeling way, when otherwise it may not be so accessible. </span>

**Use the following sequence as a model for making your own first rudimentary pattern language for the neighborhood -- just to get started. Although the following may seem "far out", it is intended to encourage you to allow free reign to your imagination at this earliest stage.**

\

<p><span class="h4class">1. It is a sequence of public squares, gardens, and buildings, which will form the new center of the city of Samarkand, uniting historic and traditional buildings and quarters.</span><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt"></span></p>
<p><span class="h4class">2. There is a new dimension here, a center of spiri­tual life. It is not a commercial center, not a cultural center, not a religious center in the old idea. It is not a convention center. Somehow, this new center of the city of Samarkand, unites old and new, weaves to­gether the thread of the silk road, the tomb of Timur the Great, with the modern world, and a vision of the world in which comfortable human concern, and a spiritual awareness of the importance of life, is vis­ible, felt, and active.<span style="LETTER-SPACING: 1pt"></span></span></p>
<p><span class="h4class">3. It is an inspiring place to go. A place of pilgrimage, which will receive visitors from the five continents, in increasing thousands.</span><strong><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Palatino Linotype&#39;; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt"></span></strong></p>
<p><span class="h4class">4. A network of beautiful paths, formed by columns, colonnades, brick walls, buildings, gardens. This network of paths, which passes across the whole area, is formed by the building masses which arise out of it, and by formal gardens.</span><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt"></span></p>
<p><span class="h4class">5. Do the paths open into courtyards, ponds, gardens, hidden places? Are they formed only by mysterious buildings, rising in color, tile, and marble? Are there figures, statues, animals, Gods, people, statues stand­ing at the places where the paths cross? </span><span style="LETTER-SPACING: 1pt"></span></p>
<p><span class="h4class">6. Are the animals themselves covered with mysteri­ous animals?</span><strong><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Palatino Linotype&#39;; LETTER-SPACING: 1pt; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt"></span></strong></p>
<p><span class="h4class">7. Is there any reference to voyages?</span><strong><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Palatino Linotype&#39;; LETTER-SPACING: 1pt; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt"></span></strong></p>
<p><span class="h4class">8. The main thing one is aware of is a network of green and beautiful jewel-like streets. Each has lush trees, seats, platforms, streams.</span></p>
<p>9. These green streets, made by their trees, benches, sitting platforms, and edges, form a lacework of places to walk. They are like parks, long and nar­row, you can explore for many hours, walking around these streets.<strong><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Palatino Linotype&#39;; LETTER-SPACING: 1pt; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt"></span></strong></p>
<p><span class="h4class">10. Each one of the streets arrives on some new trea­sure. Each building is like a treasure, arrived at by the green streets.</span><strong><span lang="EN-US" style="COLOR: #211d1e; FONT-FAMILY: &#39;Palatino Linotype&#39;; LETTER-SPACING: 1pt; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt"></span></strong></p>
<p><span class="h4class">11. Samarkand, historically, and in the time of Ulugh Beg, was a crossroads of the world. <span class="h404class"> SUMMARY OF TASKS FOR THIS UNFOLDING:</span>

------------------------------------------------------------------------

<span class="h4class" align="left"> </span>

- **Write a poem** like the one for Samarkand, for your own new, imagined neighborhood. Allow yourself free reign, free imagination, and make it poetically whole. Capture the spirit of the very best, and most serious that this new neighborhood could be.
- If possible, **pin up the poem** you have written, on the wall where people can see it, and listen to what they say.
- Put a copy in your workbook!
