<td style="text-align: left;" width="540" data-valign="top" data-bgcolor="ffffff"><blockquote>
<p><span class="h3greenclass">The following list of steps presents the unfoldings roughly in the order in which they will, typically, be used. </span></p>
<p>There are three different kinds of unfoldings.</p>
<ol>
<li>Those which are marked with the rainbow symbol <img src="../images/rainbow3.jpg" width="15" />. These inspire the design to move in a certain direction. They are unfoldings of a kind, but the unfolding occurs as a dream or image, which does not directly impact the concrete geometry of the plan. You may think of these as nascent unfoldings, which are carried forward in the process, remembered, and made concrete later.</li>
<li>Those which are marked with the house symbol <img src="../images/house2.gif" width="15" />, are the ones which play the active part in the unfolding process. That is to say, in the geometrical creation of the emerging design Each of these steps, contributes a definite transformation to the emerging design, or drawing, or computer drawing on the drawpad. It is these steps which unfold the plan and design, and cause it to be successful, or not.They are the backbone of the list.</li>
<li>Those which are unmarked and on a gray background, are also important, but less so. They do not have an immediate transformative effect on the emerging design. The provide an overall feeling, which should contribute to your understanding, and to discussion in your group. But they do not have the same unmistakable, concrete, practical effect on the design as the other two.</li>
</ol>
</blockquote></td>
