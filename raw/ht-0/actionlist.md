The process of using these unfoldings will allow the design, layout, and structure of the neighborhood to take form gradually until it is complete.</em> </span></p>

<p><span id="startup"></span>The unfoldings you have chosen will help you to implement a chain of actions that allow a living neighborhood to grow step by step.</p>
