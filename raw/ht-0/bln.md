<td colspan="3"><span class="h4class">The goal is to make your neighborhood a place of belonging, a place of health and well-being, and a place where people will want to live and work. </span>
<p>The tools offered are intended for the use of private individuals, families, communities, developers, planners, architects, designers and builders; public officials, local representatives, and neighbors; business owners and people who have commercial interests. These tools help people build and/or rebuild neighborhoods in ways that contribute something to their lives. Many of the tools have their origin in 30 years of work published in Christopher Alexander's <a href="../ht-2/noo-buy.htm"><em>The Nature of Order</em></a><br />

<td width="80%" data-valign="top">WHO ARE YOU?
<p><span class="h4class">If you are a <strong>developer</strong> or <strong>landowner</strong>, this website can show you ways of working that are more capable of creating healthy, hospitable and beautiful places for people to live, than present forms of practice typically allow. It does not cost more.</span></p>
<p>If your <strong>family</strong> or <strong>business</strong> is helping to rebuild an old neighborhood, or moving to a new one, this will give you tools and a voice which will be heard, so you can genuinely influence what happens.</p>
<p>If you are a <strong>community organizer</strong>, or a <strong>leader</strong> in a local community, this will give you tools to be sure that residents and local businesses can play an effective and practical part in decision making, which will be heard.</p>
<p>If you are a <strong>mayor</strong>, <strong>city manager</strong>, <strong>local official</strong> or <strong>city planner</strong> who wants to help create a vibrant addition to your town or city, this will show you how to support and become involved in such a process so it can be effective.</p>
<p>If you are a <strong>builder</strong> or a <strong>craftsperson</strong>, this site will show you a way of offering your services that allows the making of beauty in buildings to be a fundamental part of your contribution -- more so than is typically allowed in the present commercial context.</p>
<p>If you are an <strong>architect</strong> or <strong>engineer</strong>, this will show you how your work can be beautiful, and helpful to the beauty and spirit of a larger neighborhood.</p>
<p>If you are a <strong>teacher</strong> or a <strong>student</strong> or an <strong>academic</strong> you will find material throughout this website that naturally forms a basis for class instruction, research, and student projects. To consider teaching the use of <a href="generative.htm">generative codes</a>, you may wish to visit the <a href="archive.htm">archive</a> page for academic papers, history and background of how they were developed.</p></td>
