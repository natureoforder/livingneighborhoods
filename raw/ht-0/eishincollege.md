<p><span class="h404class">POINTS OF SPECIAL INTEREST IN THIS PROJECT</span></p>
<p>The pattern language (<a href="../library/eishin-pl-short.htm">given under Eishin Campus</a>), was made into a generative code by several important features of the process we used.</p>
<ul>
<li>We ran the construction</li>
<li>several hundred teachers and students played a role in generating the pattern language</li>
<li>we laid out the campus with the teachers and students, in the field, with flags</li>
<li>we continuously made full-size mockups during construction, and checked these on site, amongst ourselves and with the teachers</li>
<li>we controlled the money</li>
<li>the design of layout and buildings changed continuously during construction</li>
<li>we ourselves undertook the site work directly and managed the process completely</li>
</ul>
.
<p>Even more important, though, was a unique form of management which we undertook in the overall aspect of the construction works.</p></td>
