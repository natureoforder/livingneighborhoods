<p><span style="font-size:16px"><strong>YOUR LIVING NEIGHBORHOODS WORKBOOK</strong><br />
<strong>To be used in conjunction with the Website</strong></span></p>
<p><span class="h4class">This workbook will help you think about and implement the Unfoldings described on the website, livingneighborhoods.org, in a step by step manner for your own project. It will also help you organize the information you generate with others. Then you will have a record that supports discussion with others -- architects, city planners, neighbors who are joining the project, or whoever you might want to explain your project to. Careful documentation can be very useful when talking with city planners, architects, engineers and other professionals - an added benefit to taking detailed notes.</span></p>
<p>The pages in this workbook correspond with each "unfolding" page on the website. The unfolding pages on the website provide direction. The workbook pages provide space in which your project can be described, and each decision you make can be recorded with its reasoning for future reference.</p>
<p>With the livingneighborhoods.org website, you will gradually build your understanding of how to grow a neighborhood. This workbook, which you can print and keep in a binder, will allow you to document each step you take. Every investment of time and energy can be described here and shared.</p>
</blockquote></td>

<span id="#assembly"></span>
<strong>Workbook Assembly</strong>
<p><span class="h4class"> </span></p>
<blockquote>
<p>To assist you with completing and documenting each unfolding, we suggest compiling this workbook in which you can gather all the information that is pertinent to your project. There are ten (?) major phases in which all the unfolding tasks are described on the website and in the table of contents below.</p>
<ol>
<li>Print the website unfolding pages directly from the website.</li>
<li>Download and print the corresponding workbook pages that apply to the phase you are ready to work on. These will help you organize each task.</li>
<li>Assemble the printed pages in a binder. For each unfolding, place the website unfolding pages after a page divider that is labeled with the number and name of the unfolding. After the website unfolding pages, place the workbook pages that correspond with that unfolding.</li>
<li>As you work with each unfolding, keep your notes and drawings centralized in this one place so that they can be shared easily with others.</li>
<li>When you work through the phases that come later in the process, the documentation from the earlier phases will be available for review. This will help you recall earlier choices and decisions in light of current questions, and consider whether they should be adjusted to serve the whole project as it moves forward.</li>
</ol>
</blockquote>
