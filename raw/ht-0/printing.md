<p><span class="h4class"><span style="font-size:14px"><strong>To use the website effectively, you need copies of various pages. Printing pages from the website, to use them, read them, study them, look at them while walking about the neighborhood site or in the comfort of your own home or office, is an essential part of the way this website works.</strong></span> </span></p>
<p><strong>To print an individual page</strong>. Every page has a print instruction near the head of the page. To print the page out, simply click print, and you will get a better job than if you use your printer to print the html page. By using the print button you will get material neatly formatted for A4 or 8.5 x 11 paper. You are free to select color or black and white.</p>
<p><strong>To print a packet of pages</strong>. Every page has a print instruction near the head of the page.
