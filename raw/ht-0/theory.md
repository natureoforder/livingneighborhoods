<span class="h5class" style="font-size:110%;color:eeee33;">SUMMARY CONTENTS OF THIS PART OF THE WEBSITE</span>

<span class="h4class">**BASICS**</span>

<span class="blackChange"><span class="h4class">Features of a [successful neighborhood](basics.htm): size, cultural and human attributes, geometric attributes, and wholeness.</span></span>

<span class="h4class">**GENERATIVE CODES** </span>

<span class="blackChange"><span class="h4class"></span></span>

When the concepts of life from *The Nature of Order* are applied to neighborhoods, a new class of sophisticated generating systems results. These systems try to ensure that life will be enhanced in a neighborhood, and deepen over time. They are called [generative codes](generative.htm). Almost everything built in the later twentieth century was planned out ahead of time in great detail. A generative process works differently. Like a biological process, the structure is built up gradually, so that a coherent whole is able to emerge,

On the Main Steps page, the subtle human interactions, and the practical technical arrangements that are at the heart of a generative process, are set out in a straightforward manner to ensure that each is covered --\> and genuine life can be achieved.

<span class="h4class">**CREATING COMMUNITY**</span>

<span class="blackChange"><span class="h4class">[Creating human community](community.htm) as a necessary and fundamental basis for neighborhood building.</span></span>

<span class="h4class">**THE MAIN STEPS OF WORK**</span>

<span class="blackChange"><span class="h4class">**HOW TO BUILD WITH GENERATIVE CODES.**  To enable you to carry out the generative approach to city planning and to the construction of designing buildings and neighborhoods, this page gives you an extended overview of the process, with detailed instructions at each stage, on how to proceed. Both the practical technical processes and the subtle human processes are set out in a straightforward manner to ensure that each is covered and hopes of genuine life in the neighborhood may gradually be achieved. They include: [phases of thought, community discussion, generative code, design, planning, building, money, craft, and overall implementation](mainsteps.htm) .</span></span>

<span class="h4class">**LIBRARY OF UNFOLDINGS**</span>\

<span class="blackChange"><span class="h4class">**THESE ARE THE ELEMENTS OF GENERATIVE CODE THAT DO ALL THE WORK.**  The **elements** of a generative code are "unfoldings". They resemble patterns, but their emphasis is on dynamic action-oriented steps by which structure is introduced into the neighborhood, thus giving it shape. From the list of unfoldings in the [Library of Unfoldings](libraryofunfoldings.htm), choose the ones that are most useful to you. Each can define, for you, one step or action in your generative sequence of unfolding.</span></span>

<span class="h4class">**EXAMPLES OF NEIGHBORHOODS** </span>

<span class="blackChange"><span class="h4class"> Examples of living neighborhoods and the generative codes which created them show how "life" takes many, extraordinarily different forms in different places of the world. Plans, discussion, and photographs of [places and neighborhoods](neighborhoods.shtml) we have built and helped to build, or are working on, to illustrate what is involved.</span></span>

<span class="h4class">**ARCHIVES** </span>

These materials provide evidence supporting the assertions on this website. We hope that this evidence will encourage more and more people, world-wide, to take part, by improving and rebuilding neighborhoods in their own regions.</span></span>
