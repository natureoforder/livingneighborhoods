<td><h1 id="building-on-relationship" data-align="center" style="margin-right:34.65pt;margin-left:42.55pt;text-align:
center"><span style="color:red">BUILDING ON RELATIONSHIP</span></h1>
<h1 id="a-note-from-chris-alexander" data-align="center" style="margin-right:34.65pt;margin-left:42.55pt;text-align:
center"><span style="font-size:14.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:22.5pt;color:red">A note from Chris Alexander</span></h1>
<p><img src="..\images\noo.gif" id="_x0000_i1028" data-border="0" width="50" height="50" /></p>
<p> </p>
<p><span style="display:none;
mso-hide:all"> </span></p>
<p><span class="h4class1"><strong><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Arial">Human relationship</span></strong></span><span class="h4class1"><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Arial">. There are two fundamentally different ways of understanding the word "relationship," when it comes to human beings.</span></span></p>
<p><span class="h4class1"><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Arial"> </span></span></p>
<p><span class="h4class1"><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Symbol;color:windowtext">·<span style="font:7.0pt &quot;Times New Roman&quot;">         </span></span></span><span class="h4class1"><span style="font-size:
10.0pt;font-family:Arial">One of these ways is </span></span><span class="h4class1"><strong><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Arial;color:blue">conventional</span></strong></span><span class="h4class1"><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Arial">: this can describe the relationships you have with a shopkeeper, or a policeman, or a banker, or, in very sad cases, with a parent, a spouse, a son or daughter. These relationships are instrumental relationships; they are typically defined by convention – by the rules of behavior as set out by custom or by society.</span></span></p>
<p><span class="h4class1"><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Arial"> </span></span></p>
<p><span class="h4class1"><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Symbol;color:windowtext">·<span style="font:7.0pt &quot;Times New Roman&quot;">         </span></span></span><span class="h4class1"><span style="font-size:
10.0pt;font-family:Arial">The other way is </span></span><span class="h4class1"><strong><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Arial;color:blue">personal</span></strong></span><span class="h4class1"><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Arial">: the essence of the relationship is that you seek, and find, a connection; you treat the other person as nearly as possible to the way you treat yourself, and you strive constantly to treat the other person more as you treat yourself. You recognize, and slowly come to feel, that you are part of the other person, and that the other person is a part of you, so that the two of you are gradually experiencing each other as an indivisible self. </span></span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:
10.0pt;font-family:Arial">This is not only something that happens with a person who is very dear to you. You can have this quality of relationship even sitting next to a person on a park bench for only a few moments, when the exchange is something </span><strong><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Arial;color:blue">real</span></strong><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Arial">.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Arial">* * *</span></p>
<p><span class="h4class1"><strong><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Arial">Oddly enough,</span></strong></span><span class="h4class1"><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Arial"> there is a connection between the process of unfolding, the use of generative codes, and the character of the human relationships you choose.</span></span></p>
<p><span class="h4class1"><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Arial"> </span></span></p>
<p><span class="h4class1"><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Symbol;color:windowtext">·<span style="font:7.0pt &quot;Times New Roman&quot;">         </span></span></span><span class="h4class1"><span style="font-size:
10.0pt;font-family:Arial">If you carefully build relationship, in the second, truer sense, one by one, step by step, with each person you encounter, then gradually the process and understanding of unfolding, will emerge, almost by itself. </span></span></p>
<p><span class="h4class1"><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Arial"> </span></span></p>
<p><span class="h4class1"><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Symbol;color:windowtext">·<span style="font:7.0pt &quot;Times New Roman&quot;">         </span></span></span><span class="h4class1"><span style="font-size:
10.0pt;font-family:Arial">I know this to be true, just from experience. Somehow the deep understanding of how things in the world unfold, emerges from each person in a group, naturally, when there relationships are real.</span></span></p>
<p><span class="h4class1"><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Arial"> </span></span></p>
<p><span class="h4class1"><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Symbol;color:windowtext">·<span style="font:7.0pt &quot;Times New Roman&quot;">         </span></span></span><span class="h4class1"><span style="font-size:
10.0pt;font-family:Arial">It is, I think, because when relationship is real, each person feels able to express what is going on without fear. There is no inhibition from mental constraint; the notion of what must happen in a process of development is not clutched, fearful or uptight, but rather what seems right and natural can flow from the situation.</span></span></p>
<p><span class="h4class1"><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Arial"> </span></span></p>
<p><span class="h4class1"><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Symbol;color:windowtext">·<span style="font:7.0pt &quot;Times New Roman&quot;">         </span></span></span><span class="h4class1"><span style="font-size:
10.0pt;font-family:Arial">That is, of course, what happens as things unfold.</span></span></p>
<p><span class="h4class1"><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Arial"> </span></span></p>
<p><span class="h4class1"><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Symbol;color:windowtext">·<span style="font:7.0pt &quot;Times New Roman&quot;">         </span></span></span><span class="h4class1"><span style="font-size:
10.0pt;font-family:Arial">It arises from acceptance of reality, without imposing mental structures.</span></span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:
10.0pt;font-family:Arial">In any case I do know from experience that when people are in this kind of relationship, understanding of unfolding then slowly pervades the situation, and the more beautiful and natural structures are allowed to appear in the land. On the other hand, if the relationships that are present are formal, or institutional, relationships then acceptance of what is real is not allowed to exist, and the resulting mesh of mental constraint is so rigid, sometimes even slightly nasty, the most ordinary things are not allowed to appear.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Arial">* * *</span></p>
<p><span class="h4class1"><strong><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Arial">Unfolding of life then appears of its own accord, almost automatically. </span></strong></span><span class="h4class1"><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Arial">Generative codes, and the unfolding which proceeds from them, then appear of their own accord, almost automatically, simply as a direct result of the personal relationships which govern people's minds. </span></span></p>
<p><strong><span style="font-size:
10.0pt;font-family:Arial;color:blue">This is a very beautiful result.</span></strong></p>
<p><span class="h4class1"><strong><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Arial">And, sadly, vice versa.</span></strong></span><span class="h4class1"><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Arial"> In institutional settings, governmental settings, many business settings, the reverse is true. Since the setting guarantees that people cannot feel for each in a way that allows true things to be felt and said, then the result, so often –far, far too often – is a scrambled mess. Intellect cannot solve it. Living environments can hardly ever be born. </span></span></p>
<p><strong><span style="font-size:
10.0pt;font-family:Arial;color:blue">This is a very sad result, but true.</span></strong></p>
<p><strong><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Arial;color:windowtext">* * *</span></strong></p>
<p><span class="h4class1"><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Arial">As I get older, what has astonished me, lately, is the dramatic speed of the effect. One beats one's head against the wall, for years, within the institutional and business context, and never quite manages to loosen it sufficiently, in human terms, to get the desired results. But if you start, without worrying about the results so much, and focus attention only on the building of these true and personal relationships, one by one, then, as if a lever in a mountain stream had unleashed a cascade of water, the process of natural and harmonious unfolding is loosened and happens as if by a miracle. </span></span></p>
<p><strong><span style="font-size:
10.0pt;font-family:Arial;color:blue">This is so strong, it is worth taking very seriously indeed, and worth putting it first. </span><span style="color:blue"></span></strong></p>
