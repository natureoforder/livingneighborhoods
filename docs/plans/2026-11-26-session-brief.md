# Living Neighborhoods session brief

It should connect to the Alexander Wiki (alexander.wiki), where it will anchor the practical "Build with Alexander" section: how to make a room, a house, a hamlet.

**Phase 1 done-check (play back to Rufus before building).** A short plan in this folder (`docs/plans/2026-11-26-plan.md`) covering:

- State of the nation: archive completeness, how much is converted (about 44–48 of 209 pages), and quality.
- **Where it lives (decided direction, 26 Nov, Rufus):** the readable edition moves into the Alexander Wiki repo as `living-neighborhoods/`, replacing the current thin pages, and the wiki refers to it directly. There is no separate site. This repo keeps the raw archive (201 MB crawl evidence, Wayback recovery, manifest) and the conversion scripts as the preservation layer, writing their Markdown output into the wiki. Rufus agreed this split (26 Nov). Images move at full size: no shrinking. Rufus will say what he wants in this session; work through the rest with him: routes and redirects, and whether the scripts move too.
- The order of work, weighing this repo's `NEXT.md` (archive hardening and Wayback recovery before conversion) against getting readable content out sooner.
- How it feeds Build with Alexander: which generative-code phases, unfoldings and case studies (Eishin, Mexicali, Fresno, West Dean) matter most.
- Reuse and permissions. Nothing is recorded yet; see Alexander Wiki Beads `alex-88s.3` and `alex-88s.4`.
