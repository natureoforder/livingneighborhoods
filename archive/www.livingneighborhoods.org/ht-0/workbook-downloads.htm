1.0 Start-up & Vision 1.1 Worksheet: Fill out a worksheet of the basic facts.
1.2 People: Slowly and deliberately, bring in people you know who will want to be involved.
1.3 Vision: Together, close your eyes, and imagine the neighborhood as in a true vision. You wait for it, and let it come of its own accord into your eyes-shut mind.
1.4 Basemap: Make a basemap of the place where the neighborhood will be.
1.5 Poetry: In words, write down a poetic vision of the place, in the most emotional language.
