Workpad

Workpad for Startup   1.1

1.1 Worksheet: Fill out a worksheet of the basic facts.

If you have already answered the questions on "Your Basic Worksheet," place that sheet here. If you have not competed the worksheet yet, please do so. It is important to have a record of your original thoughts about your project - this is the beginning of the unfolding of your dream. If you have not completed the worksheet yet, you will find it on the first unfolding page under Start-Up/Vision.

Alternatively, you can summarize the information you submitted on the Worksheet. In particular, say as much as you can about your ideas concerning what the place would be like, feel like, and grow into, as the questions on the worksheet lead you to consider.

(See First Sketch of your Idea website page 1.1)
