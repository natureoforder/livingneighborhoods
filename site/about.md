# About this edition

This project preserves and reorganizes the [Living Neighbourhoods website](https://www.livingneighborhoods.org/), a valuable collection of Christopher Alexander's work that is currently difficult to browse and search.

## Two layers

The `archive/` directory contains a direct crawl of the original site's HTML pages and linked assets, including images, PDFs, JavaScript, and other supporting files. The crawl metadata records the source URL, date, downloaded files, and links that were unavailable when the archive was made.

The `site/` directory is the Flowershow publication. Its HTML pages have been converted to Markdown, and the original navigation has been used as a guide for a cleaner structure. The publication may be edited and improved over time; the archive remains the reference snapshot.

## Source and attribution

The source material is published at [livingneighborhoods.org](https://www.livingneighborhoods.org/). Please consult the original site's copyright and terms-of-use notices for rights and attribution questions.
